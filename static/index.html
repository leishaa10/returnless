AI-Powered Pre-Shipment Return Risk Intelligence

Predict Returns. Reduce Costs. Increase Profits.

ReturnLess empowers e-commerce retailers and D2C brands to identify high-risk product returns before dispatch. Intercept bad shipments, eliminate reverse logistics waste, and protect your bottom line.

AI The ReturnLess Pre-Shipment Decision Loop
Presentation Architecture
Step 01
Order Data Ingestion
Captures customer return history, ticket size, category sensitivity, and transit distance.
Step 02
Predictive Scoring
Rule-based explainable AI calculates return probability (0-100%) and factor weights.
Step 03
Risk Classification
Automated tiering into Low (0-39%), Medium (40-69%), or High Risk (70-100%).
Step 04
Preventive Action
Prompts WhatsApp sizing checks, address verification, or partial COD prepayments.

Why Leading E-Commerce Brands Rely on ReturnLess

Reverse logistics drains up to 30% of e-commerce margins. ReturnLess fixes the problem before the box leaves the warehouse.

Reduce Return Costs

Save hundreds of dollars per high-risk order in reverse freight, repackaging fees, and restocking overhead.

Improve Profit Margins

Prevent inventory lock-up during peak seasons so popular stock remains available for committed customers.

Optimize Logistics

Route shipments based on courier reliability, transit distances, and local delivery delivery success benchmarks.

Data-Driven Decisions

Transparent scoring weights and explainable factors turn returns from guesswork into controllable unit economics.
